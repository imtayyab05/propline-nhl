How picks are graded

  • Goals: 1+ goal (anytime). Multi-Goal: 2+ goals, in the Goals board's order.
  • Points: 1+ point. 2+ Points: 2+ points, in the Points board's order. Assists: 1+ assist. PP Points: 1+ power-play point.
  • Shots on goal: each player against the lines his board shows, his safe line and, separately, his aggressive line. Players with no line are not graded.
  • Player props count regulation and overtime; shootout attempts are not goals or shots. A player who did not play is void, not a miss.
  • Total Goals: only games that showed a MODEL LEAN badge, graded on that side of the market total. Team Goals: tagged teams against their tag (Over 2.5 / 3.5), and every team with a market team total against that line (over). Moneyline: the stronger side won. Puck Line: the stronger side covered its puck line. All of these use the final score including overtime and the shootout (the shootout winner is credited one goal, as in the official score). A result exactly on the line is a push, and a postponed game is void.
  • Top 5 / 10 / 25 are the highest-scored picks on that board each night. Base rate is the same outcome across everyone the board listed (for example the share of all listed skaters who scored); for moneyline and puck line it is how the market favourite did, for the leans how often games went that side of the line.
  • Not graded: Streaks, Head-to-Head and DvP (information tabs), and the SOG and PPG game and team boards, which no book offers a line for.
